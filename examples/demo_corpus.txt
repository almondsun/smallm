Research begins with a question. A careful experiment records its assumptions, controls, and evidence.
Small models make each decision visible: text becomes tokens, tokens become training blocks, and blocks become predictions.
Validation guides checkpoint selection. A sealed test remains untouched until every decision is frozen.
Reproducibility requires configuration, provenance, metrics, checksums, and candid interpretation.
An unexpected result is still useful when the method is inspectable and the limitations are explicit.
